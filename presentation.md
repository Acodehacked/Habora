# Habora
## Your life, in one place

A personal management platform for everything you own, subscribe to, maintain, and pay for.

---

## 1. The Problem

People manage important household information across disconnected places:

- Bills and subscriptions are spread across email, banking apps, and calendars.
- Warranties, receipts, manuals, and ownership records are lost in folders or physical paperwork.
- Repairs and maintenance responsibilities are easy to forget.
- Important renewal dates can lead to missed deadlines, unnecessary costs, and avoidable stress.

There is no simple, focused place that connects personal assets, recurring responsibilities, payments, warranties, and documents together.

**Why it matters:** scattered information costs people time, money, and peace of mind.

---

## 2. Target Customer

### Primary customers

- Homeowners managing properties, appliances, repairs, bills, and warranties.
- Tenants managing household items, subscriptions, documents, and maintenance responsibilities.
- Families sharing responsibility for household records and recurring tasks.
- Individuals who want one organized place for their personal assets and commitments.

### Customer need

They want to know:

- What do I own?
- What am I paying for?
- What needs attention next?
- When does a warranty, subscription, or document expire?
- Where is the receipt, contract, or proof of ownership?

---

## 3. Market and Competitors

### Existing alternatives

- **Google Drive:** useful for storing files, but does not organize ownership, reminders, or recurring costs.
- **Notion:** flexible for notes and databases, but requires users to design and maintain their own system.
- **Spreadsheets and calendars:** useful for individual tasks, but disconnected from documents and assets.
- **Physical folders and email:** familiar, but difficult to search, share, and keep current.
- **HomeZada:** provides broader home management, but can feel more complex and property-focused than the everyday personal management problem Habora addresses.

### Market opportunity

People already spend money on homes, appliances, vehicles, subscriptions, repairs, insurance, and services. Habora organizes the information and recurring responsibilities around those purchases in one focused experience.

**Market gap:** a simple personal platform that connects assets, services, payments, reminders, warranties, and documents.

---

## 4. Unique Value Proposition

> **Habora is the calm, simple home for everything you own, pay for, maintain, and need to remember.**

Unlike general-purpose storage or note-taking tools, Habora connects:

- Assets and ownership records
- Recurring bills and subscriptions
- Warranties and renewal dates
- Repairs and maintenance schedules
- Receipts and important documents
- Reminders for what needs attention next

Habora turns scattered records into a clear, actionable personal system.

## 6. Minimum Viable Product

The first version of Habora focuses on one complete, valuable workflow: helping a user record what they own and remember what needs attention.

### MVP features

- Email account registration and sign-in
- Personal workspace
- Asset records with categories
- Properties, appliances, vehicles, subscriptions, and documents
- Notes and optional amounts
- Reminder creation with due dates and recurrence
- Dashboard with upcoming responsibilities
- Search and filtering
- Mark reminders as complete
- Delete and manage records
- Secure Supabase authentication
- Row-level security so users can access only their own data

### MVP success criteria

A new user can:

1. Create an account.
2. Add an asset such as an appliance or subscription.
3. Add a reminder connected to a responsibility.
4. Return later and find the information quickly.
5. Complete the reminder when the task is done.
